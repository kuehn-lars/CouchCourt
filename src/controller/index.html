SwingCourt

This phone is your racket.

Hold it like a handle Top edge pointing up the racket. Swing from the shoulder.
Forehand sends it left Swing across from your racket side.
Backhand sends it right Swing back the other way, from across your body.
Swing twice to serve Once to toss the ball up, then again to hit it.

The motion sensors only talk to the screen you are playing on.

Settings

Screen flash Light up the screen when you hit or win a point.
Haptic tick A tap you can feel on each hit, where the phone allows it.
Stroke readout Shows which stroke the phone reads while you swing.
